KeySaku Typography Spec

Style: friendly, rounded, readable, playful but clean.
Recommended font stack: ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif.
Optional brand/display fonts for design exports only: Nunito, Baloo 2, Plus Jakarta Sans.

Usage:
- Display: onboarding, landing hero, reward and level-up moments.
- Heading: page titles, dashboard summary, card titles.
- Body: transaction details, helper text, descriptions.
- Label: buttons, badges, tabs, chips.

Tone:
- Indonesian UI copy.
- Short, encouraging, and action-oriented.
- Avoid childish finance wording; keep trust and clarity.
